# Code of Conduct

ClipForge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (the owner of this repository). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participants who break these rules.
